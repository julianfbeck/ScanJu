Build 1.0 (2): no automatic four-minute cutoff or app-imposed heat/memory stop. Low storage retains saved capture data. Complete ZIP exports now support ZIP64.

Please test on a LiDAR-equipped iPhone/iPad, or use Close-up on a device supporting front TrueDepth capture and world tracking. iOS/iPadOS 17 or later is required.

1. Start Complete + Detail and allow camera access. Slowly scan the floor, walls, corners, and ceiling. Check whether the filled mesh and captured/unscanned indicator make coverage clear.
2. Finish and check the processing progress, textured model, and any room measurements. Try Surface for a partial area such as a ceiling.
3. Try Close-up on a stationary object at 20–60 cm. Face the screen side toward it and keep the rear camera unobstructed. The 1.5 mm Detail fusion grid is a sampling setting, not a sensor accuracy guarantee.
4. Export through AirDrop or Save to Files. Check the OBJ/MTL/JPEG, USDZ, PLY, and available floor-plan files in your own 3D tools.
5. Continue a test scan beyond four minutes, verify that the timer and capture keep running, and finish manually. Test larger exports in ZIP64-compatible tools.
6. For recovery, close ScanJu during an unimportant test capture, reopen it, and choose Finish saved scan. Very recent data may not have reached a checkpoint.

Please report device model, iOS version, mode, scan duration, processing stage, and any crashes or missing textures through TestFlight. Attach captures only if you choose to share their contents. There is no fixed capture duration limit. Low storage stops capture with checkpoints retained; memory warnings flush caches. iOS can still interrupt recording. Complete ZIP supports ZIP64; USDZ retains its format limits. Test results cover short stationary views and forced-termination recovery; full-room dimensional accuracy and long moving captures still need broader testing.
