# ScanJu privacy policy

Updated October 18, 2026. Developer: Julian Beck. Contact: eyevo@julianbeck.com.

ScanJu captures camera images, depth maps, calibration, point clouds, meshes, and available room measurements to create 3D models. ScanJu processes and stores these on your device. It has no account, advertising, analytics service, or developer-operated cloud storage and does not upload your scans to the developer.

Camera images and scan data may contain people, belongings, and details of your home. Only capture and share material you are comfortable storing or exporting.

Camera permission enables scanning and texture capture. Rear LiDAR provides depth on supported devices. Close-up uses the front TrueDepth camera for object depth and color, while the rear camera assists world tracking. ScanJu does not use Face ID authentication, create biometric identity templates, or record face landmarks or expressions. Camera images can still contain faces if you point the camera at a person.

You choose when to export using the system share sheet or Files. Export packages contain camera images and raw capture data as well as processed models. Recipients and services you choose handle those copies under their own policies. Your device backup settings may include ScanJu's local data.

Delete all scans removes scans, textures, meshes, exports, and interrupted recordings from this app installation. It does not delete copies you exported or backed up elsewhere. You can manage app data and device backups through Apple's device settings.

When installed through TestFlight, Apple's beta-testing service may share crash reports, usage information, and tester feedback with the developer under Apple's TestFlight terms. ScanJu includes no additional analytics SDK. Feedback can be submitted through TestFlight or the contact email above. Include scan data only when you choose to share it for troubleshooting.

The app includes this privacy information in Scanning guide → Your data → Privacy and contact. This file is the source copy for a public privacy-policy page; a hosted URL has not yet been configured.
